## Color contrast tool

WCAG 2.0 AA requires a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text. This tool renders every
single-color variable from `lib/variables.css` as a swatch. Click any swatch to use it as the
comparison background — every other swatch's contrast ratio and PASS/FAIL badge recomputes live
against it, so you can find combinations that meet AA before wiring them into CSS.
